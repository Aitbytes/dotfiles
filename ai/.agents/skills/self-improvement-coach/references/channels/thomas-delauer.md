# Thomas DeLauer — Channel Deep-Dive

## Channel Overview

Thomas DeLauer is a nutrition and performance optimization expert who transformed himself from a 300+ pound corporate executive into a lean, metabolically healthy coach. His channel focuses on **intermittent fasting, ketogenic diets, metabolism, fat loss, and inflammation management**, all filtered through a lens of practical science. DeLauer's approach is notable for its willingness to evolve: he openly updates his recommendations as new research emerges, frequently publishing "changes to keto in 2025" or "new rules for intermittent fasting" videos that course-correct earlier positions. His philosophy centers on using fasting and nutritional ketosis as **stressors that trigger beneficial adaptations**, not as permanent lifestyle dogmas. He emphasizes cycling between sympathetic (fasting/stress) and parasympathetic (feeding/recovery) states, and increasingly warns against the metabolic slowdown that comes from chronic caloric restriction masquerading as fasting.

## What Makes Him Unique

- **Practical evolution over dogma**: Unlike many health influencers who defend their original positions, DeLauer regularly publishes "what's changed" updates, admitting when the science has shifted beneath his feet. His 2025 IF guide explicitly reverses several of his 2018 recommendations.
- **Metabolic flexibility as the North Star**: While he built his platform on keto and IF, his ultimate goal is metabolic flexibility — the ability to efficiently switch between fat and carbohydrate burning. This frames keto/fasting as tools, not identities.
- **Stress-adaptation paradigm**: He frames fasting, keto, cold exposure, and exercise through an allostatic load model: apply stress, then recover. This is his intellectual bridge between hardcore biohackers and longevity-focused critics like Peter Attia and Rhonda Patrick who have backed away from aggressive fasting.
- **Deep dives into mechanism**: Despite his "YouTube thumbnail" presentation style, his content goes deep into AMPK, mTOR, catecholamines, NRF2, and other pathways, often citing specific studies.
- **Strong supplementation guidance**: He provides very specific supplement protocols for both fasted and fed states, including lesser-known compounds like TMG (trimethylglycine), yohimbine/rauwolscine, and MOTS-c peptide.

## Recurring Themes & Frameworks

1. **Burst Fasting**: Instead of daily intermittent fasting, cycle 3–5 consecutive fasting days followed by 3–5 non-fasting days to prevent metabolic adaptation.
2. **Protein-First Approach**: 40–50% of calories from protein, especially on keto, to prevent muscle loss — directly addressing the Attia/Patrick critique of fasting.
3. **Early Time-Restricted Feeding (eTRF)**: Eating earlier in the day and fasting through the afternoon/evening for better sleep and hormonal health.
4. **The Food Pyramid**: Lean meats at the base → fattier cuts/eggs → dairy/aged cheeses → fruit/honey → veggies/starches → strategic fats → treats.
5. **Monounsaturated Fat Emphasis**: Shifting keto fat sources toward MUFA (eggs, duck, pork, macadamia nuts, olive oil) and away from heavy saturated fat and seed oils.
6. **Sympathetic/Parasympathetic Cycling**: Fasting is a sympathetic stressor; recovery is parasympathetic. Don't fast when life stress is already high.
7. **HIIT/Resistance During Fasts**: Prioritize resistance training (not just cardio) during fasts to signal muscle preservation to the body.

### 1. How to do Intermittent Fasting: Complete Guide (2018)

- **URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LLVf3d0rqqY
- **Duration**: ~25 minutes (1522s)
- **Published**: November 2018
- **Why This Matters**: This is the **foundational artifact** of DeLauer's channel — the video that established him as the go-to IF explainer on YouTube. It's his most comprehensive single IF overview, covering everything a beginner needs to start. Though some recommendations have evolved (see #4 below), the core framework remains the backbone of his philosophy.
- **Key Takeaways**:
  - Intermittent fasting is a **meal timing plan, not a diet** — it works with keto, paleo, vegan, or any eating pattern.
  - The **16:8 split** (16h fasting, 8h eating window) is the entry point; autophagy and cellular rejuvenation kick in meaningfully after 16 hours.
  - **Black coffee is fasting-friendly** and actually enhances autophagy via polyphenol-driven AMPK activation (citing a study in *Cell Cycle*).
  - **Artificial sweeteners can trigger cephalic insulin responses** — prefer stevia, avoid sucralose.
  - **Never combine fats and carbs when breaking a fast**: carbs spike insulin → cells open → dietary fat gets stored alongside glucose. Break fasts with lean protein first, then add either fats OR carbs (not both) 30–90 minutes later.
  - **Work out fasted**: exercise in the fasted state mobilizes intramyocellular lipids (fat droplets inside muscle) for fuel, accelerating fat loss.
  - **Bone broth to break a fast**: the collagen helps restore the gut mucosal layer weakened during fasting.
  - **Thyroid concerns are overblown**: thyroid hormone production may slow during the fast but recovers immediately upon refeeding; the thyroid gland is highly regenerative.
  - Women should start slower (12–14h fasts) due to stronger ghrelin/hunger signaling from reproductive biology, but can ultimately benefit equally.
  - **Dry fasting** (no food or water) pulls hydrogen from fat stores to create metabolic water, but should only be done every 3–6 months.
- **Studies/Books/Channels Cited**:
  - *Journal of Translational Medicine* — study showing IF subjects burned more fat AND built more muscle vs. calorie-matched controls
  - *Cell Cycle* — study on coffee polyphenols enhancing autophagy
  - *Journal of Endocrinology* — study on thyroid function during fasting (TSH drops temporarily, recovers on refeeding)
  - *Journal of Physiology* — study showing fasted exercise burns more intramyocellular lipid

### 2. How to Start Intermittent Fasting in 2023 — UPDATED INFORMATION & SCIENCE

- **URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OiGg4Fk1PGY
- **Duration**: ~36 minutes (2196s)
- **Published**: February 2023
- **Why This Matters**: This is the **major mid-career update** where DeLauer course-corrects several 2018-era positions. It introduces eTRF (early time-restricted feeding), alternate-day fasting, and the critical concept that **fasting should be the anomaly, not the norm**. This video marks his pivot away from "fast every day" toward "fast in bursts." It's arguably the most important watch for anyone who was following his older advice.
- **Key Takeaways**:
  - **Don't fast every day**: Take at least 2 days off per week from IF. Daily 16:8 just becomes chronic caloric restriction, which slows metabolism.
  - **Early Time-Restricted Feeding (eTRF)** is the new gold standard: eat earlier (stop by 2–3 PM), fast through the evening. This aligns with circadian biology and improves sleep, libido, and hormone health.
  - **Alternate-day fasting** (24h fast, 24h eat, repeat) shows the strongest results in animal and human studies — it's the "flexible dieting of the fasting world."
  - **OMAD (one meal a day) is problematic daily** but effective 2–3x/week as a metabolic shock. Daily OMAD crushes metabolism and often leads to nutritional deficiencies.
  - **Break fasts with protein, then wait 30–90 min before carbs**. Protein provides 1g per pound of target lean body mass. High-glycemic carbs immediately upon breaking a fast spike insulin and disrupt electrolytes.
  - **Fasting superfoods**: Green tea (increases REE during fasts), turmeric (fasting mimetic — extends fast-like effects into the eating window), avocado (MUFA, satiety via PYY/GLP-1), macadamia nuts (omega-7s, insulin sensitivity), high-omega-3 fish (improves mTOR phosphorylation for muscle protein synthesis).
  - **Organic turkey** is ideal post-fast protein due to high phosphorus and thiamine, two nutrients depleted during longer fasts.
  - **Supplements during fasts**: Electrolytes (always), 2g creatine (hydration/cognition), tyrosine (fuel switching), GABA (calmness), acetyl-L-carnitine (crosses BBB to fuel brain with fat), rhodiola rosea (adaptogen), glutamine (gut health).
  - **Supplements during eating window**: Antioxidants (vitamin C, astaxanthin, haskap berry, glutathione, sulforaphane), EAAs with protein (up to 4x synthesis boost), cod liver oil (vitamin D + omega-3 + retinol), CoQ10 (300–600mg), tongkat ali (for men: estrogen control/testosterone).
  - **Signs you're fasting too much**: not hungry during fasts, weak, losing menstrual regularity (women), breaking fasts with junk food without consequences.
  - **Ghrelin is beneficial**: the hunger hormone itself drives many of fasting's adaptive benefits. If you never feel hungry during a fast, your metabolism has likely adapted.
- **Studies/Books/Channels Cited**:
  - *Journal of Translational Medicine* — study on IF correcting PCOS: 73% of women normalized menstrual cycles after 5 weeks
  - References to early time-restricted feeding literature (circadian biology + metabolism)
  - Mentions Dr. Andrew Huberman re: eating too close to bedtime disrupting sleep
  - Thrive Market partnership — Mediterranean-style diet framework

### 3. Major Changes to Keto in 2025 — New Guide to Lose Fat and Build Muscle

- **URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XWyrPP7XJhg
- **Duration**: ~22 minutes (1350s)
- **Published**: January 2025
- **Why This Matters**: This is DeLauer's **definitive modern keto framework**, representing his most evolved thinking on the ketogenic diet. It directly addresses why keto "fell by the wayside" (dirty keto, excessive saturated fat, no carb cycling) and provides a clean, sustainable 2025 protocol. The shift toward 50% protein and emphasis on MUFAs over saturated fat is a significant departure from traditional keto macro ratios.
- **Key Takeaways**:
  - **Keto is about driving up ketones, not just alienating carbs**. Ketones have anti-inflammatory, neuroprotective, and cognitive benefits — the diet is a tool, not just a fat-loss hack.
  - **50% protein is the new keto macro**: traditional 70–80% fat keto is outdated. Higher protein prevents muscle loss (addressing the Attia/Patrick critique) and slightly suppresses ketone formation, compensated by choosing ketone-promoting fats.
  - **Fat breakdown: 20% saturated, 20% monounsaturated, 10–15% polyunsaturated**. Get MUFAs from eggs, duck, pork loin, macadamia nuts, olive oil — NOT from seed oils. Saturated fat from dairy (aged cheeses rich in C15:0) rather than just fatty meat.
  - **C15:0 (pentadecanoic acid)** from aged cheeses is critical: it adds structural scaffolding to cell membranes, preventing "cellular fragility syndrome" common in long-term keto dieters consuming only oils.
  - **Cycle keto with PSMF (protein-sparing modified fast)**: 1 week high-fat keto → 3–4 days very low-fat, high-protein keto → repeat. This forces the body to pull from its own fat stores.
  - **Commit 60–90 days to full adaptation OR use intra-workout carbs**: 2 tbsp honey during workouts leverages insulin-independent glucose uptake to restore muscle glycogen without leaving ketosis (~50g carbs OK).
  - **Occasionally eat small carb boluses** (15–20g honey/maple syrup every other day) to maintain glucose tolerance — "if you don't use it, you lose it."
  - **Fiber still matters on keto**: 30–35g/day. Butyrate from fiber fermentation supports ketone formation. Mid-range fiber is worse than high fiber or no fiber (carnivore).
  - **Key supplements**: TMG (trimethylglycine) for methylation/homocysteine/muscle preservation, creatine (5–15g — anti-inflammatory for brain), yohimbine/rauwolscine (alpha-2 adrenergic antagonist — unlocks fat cell release), MOTS-c peptide (mitochondrial exercise mimetic — experimental).
  - **Sweetener guidance**: Erythritol and xylitol are fine (the recent negative studies were reverse causation/flawed design). Prefer monk fruit, stevia, allulose. Allulose uniquely blocks glucose/fructose absorption via GLUT5 transporter occupancy.
- **Studies/Books/Channels Cited**:
  - References to C15:0 research (cellular fragility syndrome — first new clinical fatty acid deficiency discovered in 75 years)
  - Work with US Special Forces (Army) and Air Force TACP on ketogenic protocols for TBI and cognitive function
  - SEED probiotic partnership — gut health on keto
  - A2 milk movement (Pioneer Pastures advisory role)
  - Dr. Rhonda Patrick and Dr. Peter Attia mentioned as critics who pivoted on fasting/keto due to muscle loss concerns

### 4. Important Changes to Intermittent Fasting in 2025 — New Guide to Lose Fat & Build Muscle

- **URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KWtSWTm-DYw
- **Duration**: ~20 minutes (1198s)
- **Published**: January 2025
- **Why This Matters**: This is DeLauer's **most current IF framework** and his direct response to prominent critics (Attia, Patrick) who have distanced themselves from fasting. He reframes the debate not as "fasting is bad" but as "we need to learn how to implement it with recovery." The burst fasting protocol and sympathetic/parasympathetic cycling model are his mature intellectual contributions to the field.
- **Key Takeaways**:
  - **Burst Fasting is the new model**: Fast 3–5 days consecutively (18h fasts, not multi-day), then take 3–5 days off completely. This prevents psychological addiction to the fasted state and metabolic adaptation.
  - **18:6 is the new 16:8**: 18 hours is where AMPK activation, gluconeogenesis, and meaningful fat mobilization kick in. 16 hours barely scratches the surface.
  - **Skip dinner, not breakfast**: Fasting through the evening and sleeping deeper into the fasted state produces more ketones and lower insulin by morning. 3–4 days/week, skip dinner; the rest of the week, eat normally.
  - **Variable calorie fasting days**: Some fasting days eat 1500 kcal, others 3000 kcal. Randomness keeps the body from adapting.
  - **Resistance training on fasting days, cardio on feeding days**: Muscle stimulus during fasting signals preservation. Cardio on fasting days is unnecessarily catabolic.
  - **Creatine at 5–15g on fasting days**: Maintains intracellular hydration, brain energy, and muscle fullness. Use fasting-friendly sweetened versions (monk fruit).
  - **Sauna/hot bath during fasts**: Exercise mimetic → fat loss + growth hormone release with no muscular work.
  - **Don't cold plunge on fasting days**: Too much allostatic load. Cold exposure is an acute stressor — pair it with feeding days, not fasting days.
  - **Don't do Zone 5 cardio fasted** (unless experienced): too much stress. Upper Zone 3 / Zone 4 is the sweet spot.
  - **Post-fast relaxation stack**: Magnesium (dimagnesium malate + glycinate), nicotinoyl GABA, honokiol (magnolia bark), tryptophan (1000mg), theanine (400mg) — all to tilt toward parasympathetic recovery.
  - **Carbs in the evening on feeding days**: Helps sleep via insulin-mediated tryptophan transport into the brain. Don't eat within 2–3 hours of bed if insulin resistant.
  - **You are in control**: don't fast when life stress is high (argument with spouse, fired from work). Allostatic load matters more than schedule adherence.
- **Studies/Books/Channels Cited**:
  - References to Dr. Peter Attia and Dr. Rhonda Patrick pivoting on fasting (the "too much stress and catabolism" critique)
  - Dr. Mike Israetel on carb timing ("doesn't matter if you binge sushi at night or spread carbs throughout the day")
  - Dr. Ben Bikman on artificial sweeteners and insulin
  - Dr. Nick Norwitz
  - Dr. Scott Sher on GABA and parasympathetic recovery (Troop product)
  - Dr. Venn Watson on C15:0 vs. fish oil

### 5. The Literal Easiest Fat Loss Diet Plan I've Ever Posted on the Channel

- **URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6UXj7yp8u6w
- **Duration**: ~11 minutes (645s)
- **Published**: January 2025
- **Why This Matters**: This is DeLauer's **simplest, most actionable fat-loss framework** — stripped of esoteric mechanisms and designed for all experience levels. It distills years of content into a food pyramid and 10 key foods. This is the video to give someone who says "just tell me what to eat." It also introduces his **high-calorie / low-calorie week cycling** concept, which is a practical implementation of his metabolic flexibility philosophy.
- **Key Takeaways**:
  - **The DeLauer Food Pyramid** (bottom to top): Lean meats (96/93% ground beef, chicken) → Fattier cuts/eggs → Dairy protein (Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, aged cheeses) → Fruit & honey → Veggies & starches (sweet potatoes, chia, flax) → Strategic fats (olive oil, macadamia nuts) → Treats (daily, in small amounts).
  - **40–50% of calories from protein** as a general rule. On higher-calorie days, protein percentage naturally decreases — don't force 50% on a 4000kcal day.
  - **High-calorie weeks + low-calorie weeks**: eat more, move more on high weeks; eat less, move less on low weeks. This maintains metabolic rate and prevents adaptation — like a "descending ladder" that maintains intensity.
  - **When in doubt, protein shake or electrolytes**: The simplest intervention when hungry or craving. Sip salt water / LMNT electrolytes — "salt kills cravings."
  - **Limit fats, not carbs, when already in a surplus**: Dietary fat stores more readily as body fat when you're in a caloric surplus than carbohydrates do. "Don't get mad at me, get mad at the science."
  - **Don't overthink glycemic index** if metabolically healthy. Focus on total glucose area under the curve across the day rather than individual spikes.
  - **The 10 Key Foods for 2025**: Eggs + egg whites, lean ground beef, honey (Manuka/acacia/wildflower), chia + flax, fermented dairy (kefir, yogurt) + fermented veggies (kimchi, sauerkraut), berries (strawberry, blueberry, blackberry), macadamia nuts, Greek yogurt + cottage cheese, sweet potatoes (purple), whey protein concentrate.
  - **Balance sympathetic and parasympathetic**: If training hard and eating in deficit simultaneously, overuse injuries and metabolic slowdown are inevitable. Pick one stressor at a time.
  - **Fermented foods are underrated**: Kefir, yogurt, kimchi, sauerkraut — gut microbiome support independent of fiber intake.
