# Self-Improvement Coach

A curated reference library synthesizing the most influential self-improvement YouTube channels, their foundational videos, the books and studies they cite, and the concepts that recur across the ecosystem.

## How to Use This Skill

1. **For domain-specific advice**: Navigate to the relevant domain section below and open the channel reference files that match the user's need.
2. **For cross-domain questions**: Consult [Cross-Cutting Concepts](references/concepts.md) first — it maps 28 frameworks that span multiple domains (e.g., progressive overload, dopamine regulation, Stoic dichotomy of control).
3. **For book recommendations**: See [Books](references/books/) — 40 books ranked by citation frequency across channels.
4. **For evidence quality**: See [Studies](references/studies/) and [Researchers](references/researchers/) for the scientific backbone.
5. **For a specific channel**: Jump to the Domain Index below, find the channel, and open its reference file.
