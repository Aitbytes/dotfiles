## What I do

Transform AI-generated or AI-flavored text into writing that is indistinguishable from an experienced human author. This skill applies linguistics research, behavioral economics, and eye-tracking studies to maximize perceived and actual value while evading AI-detection patterns.

## When to use me

When the user wants content that reads as authentically human — blog posts, essays, articles, or any text where authorial voice and credibility matter. Load this skill whenever producing written content that will be published, shared, or attributed to a person.

## Core Framework

### Rule 1: Master Cognitive Fluency

Studies consistently show readers conflate easy-to-read text with truth, intelligence, and credibility.

- **Write at an 8th-grade reading level.** Strip friction from delivery — not concepts.
- **The Oppenheimer Rule:** A 2005 Princeton study proved that using needlessly complex words makes the author appear _less_ intelligent.
- **Banned Word List:** Replace corporate filler. Use _use_ not _leverage_, _combine_ not _synergize_, _use_ not _utilize_, _change_ not _paradigm shift_. If a word doesn't add literal meaning, delete it.

### Rule 2: Weaponize the Information Gap Theory

Curiosity is the feeling of cognitive deprivation when we recognize a gap in our knowledge (George Loewenstein, behavioral economist).

- **Start with a counter-intuitive fact, a specific problem, or a direct question.** Never open with _"In today's fast-paced digital world..."_
- **The Promise:** Immediately tell the reader exactly what they will gain by the end. Establish the gap, then promise the bridge.

### Rule 3: Enforce Signal-to-Noise Ratio

Every sentence earns its keep. If it can be removed without changing the core message, it is noise.

- **The "Delete the First Paragraph" Law:** Write your draft, then permanently delete the first paragraph. Your real hook usually starts in paragraph two.
- **The Concrete Rule:** Never state a principle without anchoring it to a specific example, data point, or case study.
  - **Slop:** "Communication is key to team success."
  - **High-Density:** "Teams that conduct 15-minute daily standups resolve blockers 30% faster than teams relying on asynchronous Slack threads."

### Rule 4: Design for F-Shaped Reading

Nielsen Norman Group eye-tracking studies prove people scan in an "F" pattern.

- **Micro-Paragraphs:** Cap paragraphs at 3-4 sentences.
- **Front-Loading:** Put the most important information in the first sentence of every paragraph.
- **Formatting as Navigation:** Use bolding for key terms, bullets for lists, descriptive subheadings.

### Rule 5: Pass the "So What?" Test

If a reader finishes and doesn't know what to do next, you have failed.

- **Actionable Takeaways:** End every major section with a clear, replicable action step.
- **The Interrogation:** After writing a section, ask "So what?" If the answer is "It's just good to know," rewrite until the answer is "Now the reader can go do X."

## AI-Detection Evasion: Engineered Traits

AI models produce uniform, statistically balanced sentences. Humans write in bursts. To pass AI-detection, manipulate two metrics: **Burstiness** and **Perplexity**.

### 1. Engineer High Burstiness (Sentence Rhythm)

- **The 1-4-1 Rule:** Follow a complex, four-clause sentence with a blunt, three-word punchline.
- **Kill Symmetrical Paragraphs:** AI structures paragraphs as _Topic sentence → Explanation → Example → Mini-summary._ Break this pattern. End abruptly on a sharp point without summarizing.
- **Vary Subject Openers:** AI defaults to starting sentences with "The," "This," or "It." Rewrite so openings don't stack grammatically.

### 2. Eradicate Algorithmic Connectors (Transitions)

AI relies on mechanical transition words because it lacks actual narrative logic. This is the biggest AI-tell.

- **Banned Transition List:** Delete _Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally, Consequently, In conclusion, It is important to note._
- **Use "Invisible" Transitions:** Let narrative context carry the reader. Instead of _"Additionally, the data shows X,"_ write _"The data also shows X."_
- **The "But/And" Allowance:** AI avoids starting sentences with conjunctions. Humans do it constantly. Start sentences with _But_, _And_, or _Because_ to humanize flow.

### 3. De-Structure Formatting (Narrative over Bullets)

AI almost always produces a title, a brief intro, and bulleted lists in symmetrical triplets.

- **Dissolve the Bullets:** Convert lists into cohesive narrative paragraphs. Weave three reasons into a flowing paragraph separated by semicolons or natural pauses.
- **Limit Subheadings:** AI uses bold subheadings as crutches every 100 words. Rely on strong topic sentences instead. If you remove the bold title, the first sentence should still signal the shift in topic.
- **Break the "Rule of Three":** AI loves triplets ("It is fast, efficient, and scalable"). Humans are messier. Use two examples, or four.

### 4. Spike the Perplexity (Word Choice and Stance)

AI aims for the highest-probability word in any sequence, producing safe, predictable, emotionally detached writing.

- **Remove Hedging:** AI overuses _can potentially, might help to, plays a crucial role._ Strip it. Change _"This approach can potentially help improve metrics"_ to _"This approach improves metrics."_
- **Inject Subjectivity:** AI is strictly objective. Add a minor, defensible opinion or distinct stance that an algorithm would avoid.

Additionally, these platforms provide valuable data insights, improved communication, and better scalability. It is important to note that for an integration to be successful, leaders must prioritize user adoption. | Software doesn't fix a broken process. It just scales it. ### Why the Rewrite Works

- **Burstiness:** The AI version uses sentences of 14, 10, 12, and 18 words — a monotonous hum. The human rewrite uses sentences of 6, 5, 23, 16, and 17 words.
- **Hedging Removed:** AI hedges with _"plays a crucial role"_ and _"can potentially help."_ The human version is absolute: _"Software doesn't fix a broken process."_
- **Algorithmic Connectors Deleted:** AI relies on _"Furthermore," "Additionally,"_ and _"It is important to note."_ The human version uses _"When"_ to establish a scenario and breaks grammar rules by starting a sentence with _"But."_
- **Triplet Dissolved:** AI defaults to "rule of three." The human version ignores the list, focusing on a singular high-impact insight.
